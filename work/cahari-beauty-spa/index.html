Cahari Beauty SpaA spa ritual, from first touch to final glow

The challenge

What needed to change.

Spa results are personal and hard to describe. People need to see the care, the products and the calm before they book.

Our approach

How we filmed it.

We followed one full facial: mixing the mask, the application, the LED mask and the finish, with the spa's name kept subtle on screen. For the opening, a short "Coming soon" teaser built anticipation before the doors opened.
