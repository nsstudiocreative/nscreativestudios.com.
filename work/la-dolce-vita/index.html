The challenge

What needed to change.

An experience-led event happens once. The content had to show what it felt like to be there, so the next one sells itself.

Our approach

How we filmed it.

We covered the arrivals, the flowers, the stage and, above all, the scent bar: hands at the dispensers, guests taking notes and bottles being finished. Two pieces came out of the night: an event recap and a reel focused on the scent bar.
