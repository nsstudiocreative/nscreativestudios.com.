Sudbury Point Grill

Reels for the menu, the kitchen, private events and delivery, all filmed on location in one restaurant.

Sudbury Point GrillA local grill, shown the way regulars see it

The challenge

What needed to change.

A full-service restaurant has a lot to say: a new healthy menu, a busy kitchen, a function room and delivery. Each one needed its own reason to stop scrolling.

Our approach

How we filmed it.

We filmed the dining room, the bar and the kitchen line, then cut a separate reel for each goal: "10 Healthy Picks" for the menu, "Everything is carefully prepared" for the kitchen, "Planning an event? Say no more" for the function room and a short delivery reel that points to DoorDash and Grubhub.
